

There are three phases of product

1. Research -> one of a kind, high capital, unproven, high risk
2. Deep tech -> n of a kind, moderate capital, semi proven, but more engineering challenges than research, moderate risk
3. Execution -> no longer unique, low capital, proven, speedrunning and iterating is all that matters. incremental advantages

# Premise

> The premise establishes why a field is in deep tech, not research or execution phase.


Classical robotics is firmly in the execution space. With AI, Semi Generalizable Robotics has just crossed over into the deep tech space in the last year and a half. Fully generalizable robotics is still in research phase.

drones, self driving cars, recent papers on Vision Language Action models have shown AI and robotics work in the real world.  

Language models followed a similar trend
1. Research phase: n-gram, seq2seq, lstm, transformer established feasibility
2. Deep tech phase: gpt 1
