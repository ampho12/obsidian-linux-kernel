


## What is my ROI and how do you get there

Venture Capital expects 100x the return. If they invest at 10 million valuation, they expect 1 billion valuation when they cash out. 

For this, they expect a plan. But we can work backwards from this.

Use P/E ratio, find how much of the market you have to capture to reach that valuation.

List steps to get there, how each step unlocks the next phase.


E.g.

1. We want to sell developer kits to 200 industry professionals and researchers.
2. This unlocks data stream and gets talent acquanted with our platform, while making it more robust.
3. We use the data and hire aggressively to build a robot for X commercial market.
4. The tasks we do at X make us an established partner, X will now let us do more tasks, while we can keep doing the task we already did at X in Y and Z markets too.





## Do not be indecisive

> Indecision = no decision

Q: what actuators are you using and why
We use robstride for the low gear ratio, robust can communication, ota and low cost.


Q: We know Encos, do you want to intro to them

Bad answer: Yes, we would love to chat. This shows indecision. A lack of conviction in your decision shows no decision at all.

Good answer (even if its is made up): last time we checked they didn't have the criteria we are looking for, but if that's changed we can talk again.

1. Unit economics
2. Financing model
3. Why X industry
4. Who are your competitors and how will you beat them.
5. How will you reach 200 robots
