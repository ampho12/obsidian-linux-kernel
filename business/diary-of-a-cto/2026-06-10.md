



1. Conviction in TAM
    1. Startup TAMs are weighted by conviction. High TAM use cases usually have lower conviction. Low TAM use cases have high conviction
2. Unlocking conviction
    1. Startups need to build conviction. Usually the wedge task, will be easier but will be the birthplace of a compounding technical advantage.
    2. The goal is to build conviction that: compounding nature will unlock larger TAM. 
        1. Find metrics that correlate with your advantage, and the task you are trying to solve.
            1. General purpose robotics: the metrics need to capture general purpose tasks
            2. Specific: show how specific tasks compound by showing same models / approach working on different tasks. Show how data collection is more tractable.

Intuitively, people know the whole pie is really big. We don't want to sub-optimize their reaction to how much the tam is. Put smaller numbers on the appendix. People tend to get nitpicky over details. Don't distract.
