
Every robotics pitch looks like
"I will use X tech with robots. Will start with Y industry, collect data to generalize "

Generalization relies on human data. The goal is to spend millions of dollars in machine learning and hardware, to match to human performance. This lower the cost of labor -- textbook scarcity mindset

Gradient's mission is to create abundance, starting with flops in data centers. Human level generalization is not the goal, we need to be superhuman to meet data center demands.

The plan
1. Add sensing (cameras,  IMUs, force sensors) to existing infrastructure. Collect efficiency data and process data. We use it to streamline operations in data centers. Deliver value on day one, and in short term.
2. We use the data to understand how things are done and build the benchmark of today's physical infrastructure. Then we build our system to beat it -- unlocking abundance
