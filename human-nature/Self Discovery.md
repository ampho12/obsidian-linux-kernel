## Acceptance and Commitment therapy

When we live in service to our values - things go well
When we live in service to desires or shoulds - things go poorly
- To engage in desire: benefit must outweight cost. Society today gives a way to get benefit (eg scrolling smartphone) with hidden costs (attention span)
- Living a life of shoulds: we can accomplish a lot, but we will burn out
